Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this application collects, uses, and protects personal information, including mobile phone numbers used for SMS-based account notifications and verification.

Information collected

We may collect your name, email address, mobile phone number, account identifiers, device information, and usage data when you register for an account or use the application.

How information is used

We use your information to create and manage your account, provide login verification codes, send important account notifications, maintain security, respond to support requests, and improve the service.

SMS messaging

If you provide your mobile number and consent to SMS, you may receive text messages related to account verification, login security, password reset, and important account notifications.

Message frequency varies based on account activity. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You can reply HELP for help.

Mobile number sharing

Mobile information will not be shared, sold, or transferred to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect personal information.

Your choices

You may update account information in the application when available. You may stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.
